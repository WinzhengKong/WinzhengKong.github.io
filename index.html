Wenzheng Jiang  |  江文钲

I am a Ph.D. student in the College of Computer Science and Technology at National University of Defense Technology (NUDT), advised by Prof. Huaimin Wang and Prof. Dawei Feng.

Before beginning my Ph.D., I received my M.S. degree from NUDT in December 2025 (early graduation) under the supervision of Prof. Ji Wang and Prof. Xiaomin Zhu, after earning my B.Eng. degree in Automation from Central South University in June 2023.

My research focuses on reliable and trustworthy AI, with particular interests in large language models, multimodal learning and autonomous agents. I also study robust and adaptive federated learning.
