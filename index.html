About me

Wenzheng Jiang 江文钲

Research interests: Reliable & Trustworthy AI; LLM & Agent. Reliable & Trustworthy AI LLM & Agent

🎓 I am a Ph.D. student in the National Key Laboratory of Parallel and Distributed Processing within the College of Computer Science and Technology at National University of Defense Technology, advised by Prof. Huaimin Wang and Prof. Dawei Feng. I am also affiliated with the State Key Laboratory of Complex & Critical Software Environment.

📚 Before beginning my Ph.D., I received my M.S. degree from National University of Defense Technology in December 2025 (early graduation) under the supervision of Prof. Ji Wang and Prof. Xiaomin Zhu at the National Key Laboratory of Big Data and Decision, after earning my B.Eng. degree in Automation from Central South University in June 2023.

🔬 My research focuses on reliable and trustworthy AI, with particular interests in large language models, multimodal learning and autonomous agents. I also study robust and adaptive federated learning.
